# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for a Block having a single
# BendersDecompositionSolver attached, in the MILP regime, separating one
# cut for both feasibility and optimality: the epigraph inequality joins the
# coupling Constraint that carry a slack in the phase one, and the cut is the
# linearization of that problem in the complicating Variable and in the
# epigraph one. The cost of the slack of the epigraph inequality, relative to
# those of the coupling Constraint, is the normalization of the separation.
#
#   Antonio Frangioni
#   Donato Meoli

# now all the names of the Solver - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
BendersDecompositionSolver # name of Solver

1  # number of ComputeConfig in this BlockSolverConfig

# now all the ComputeConfig
# 1st ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

ComputeConfig # exact type of the ComputeConfig object

   # f_diff == 1 ==> all non-provided parameters are not changed

int_BDSlv_Regime  1  # 0 = convex master, 1 = MILP master
