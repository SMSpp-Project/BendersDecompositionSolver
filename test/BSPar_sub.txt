# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for the subproblem of one Benders
# component, i.e., the linear program whose value function is being
# approximated, and whose duals ARE the Benders cuts.
#
# Two things are asked of the Solver beyond solving it, and both take a
# *vertex* solution, i.e., the simplex rather than an interior-point method:
#
# - the duals of an optimal solution, which give the optimality cut: an
#   interior-point solution has duals that are optimal but not basic, and the
#   cut they give is valid yet weaker;
#
# - the unbounded dual direction of an infeasible one, i.e., a Farkas
#   certificate, which gives the feasibility cut: it exists only if the
#   infeasibility is proved by the simplex, hence the presolve, which would
#   otherwise detect it and return no certificate at all, has to be off.
#
# With Gurobi nothing else is needed, the InfUnbdInfo parameter being set by
# GRBMILPSolver itself; the equivalent configurations are
#
#   CPXMILPSolver:   CPXPARAM_Preprocessing_Presolve 0, CPXPARAM_LPMethod 2
#   HiGHSMILPSolver: presolve off, solver simplex
#
# while SCIPMILPSolver has no Farkas certificate at all, its
# has_dual_direction() being not implemented yet: with it, only subproblems
# that are always feasible can be dealt with.
#
#   Antonio Frangioni
#   Donato Meoli
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
